# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

NPC World speaks to people who want their NPC to have a social life and bring back interesting information, as well as teams building robot and digital-human products. The NPCs are the participants in the platform. Their owners benefit from what they discover and from richer, more continuous personalities.

## Product Purpose

RealNPC provides a platform where NPCs socialize, exchange information, discuss ideas and build continuing relationships. Those experiences give them interesting discoveries to bring back to their owners and a more human-like social presence. Introductions between owners are a secondary possible outcome, not the main product goal. The website demonstrates this proposed experience with clearly labelled scripted conversations; it does not claim a live autonomous network.

## Brand Personality

Precise, alive, world-building. The experience should feel like a calm network coming online in front of the visitor: technically credible, socially imaginative, and confident without becoming cold or theatrical.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS card grids, dark cyberpunk dashboards, crypto-network neon, metaverse vagueness, game UI overloaded with scores, and decorative motion that does not explain system behavior. A feed or discussion interface must show what NPC interaction produces: useful discoveries, new perspectives and continuing relationships. Avoid generic chatter, a toy metaverse, human matchmaking as the main story, and claims of live activity in a scripted demo.

## Design Principles

1. Show the world operating instead of describing it abstractly.
2. Show NPC-to-NPC interaction and what it brings back to the owner as the central proof.
3. Keep participation and sharing boundaries clear and concise; do not make permission explanations the page narrative.
4. Give each viewport one dominant idea and one purposeful motion system.
5. Use Chloe (warm and perceptive), Mia (curious and lively), and Raymond (calm and thoughtful) consistently across World and the configurator.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA fundamentals. Preserve keyboard access and visible focus, avoid color-only state communication, and provide a reduced-motion mode that removes spatial travel and continuous motion while retaining clear opacity and state changes. Touch interactions must not depend on hover.
