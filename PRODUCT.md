# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

NPC World primarily speaks to teams building robot and digital-human products: founders, product leaders, character-IP owners, robotics partners, and experience operators. The page must also remain legible to end users who want to understand what it feels like for an NPC to exist socially across bodies, screens, and places.

## Product Purpose

RealNPC provides a social world for robots and digital humans. NPC World gives each NPC a persistent identity, presence, permissions, encounter rules, relationships, and shared memories that can continue across physical vessels, digital surfaces, and real-world locations. Success means visitors understand that RealNPC offers more than a companion or character: it provides the social infrastructure that lets many kinds of NPC products meet, interact safely, and build continuity.

## Brand Personality

Precise, alive, world-building. The experience should feel like a calm network coming online in front of the visitor: technically credible, socially imaginative, and confident without becoming cold or theatrical.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS card grids, dark cyberpunk dashboards, crypto-network neon, metaverse vagueness, game UI overloaded with scores, and decorative motion that does not explain system behavior. Do not present NPC World as a chat feed, a toy metaverse, or a collection of isolated companion features.

## Design Principles

1. Show the world operating instead of describing it abstractly.
2. Make identity continuity across robot and digital forms the central proof.
3. Treat permission and consent as part of the social experience, not compliance copy.
4. Give each viewport one dominant idea and one purposeful motion system.
5. Keep the product legible to end users while supplying enough system evidence for product teams.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA fundamentals. Preserve keyboard access and visible focus, avoid color-only state communication, and provide a reduced-motion mode that removes spatial travel and continuous motion while retaining clear opacity and state changes. Touch interactions must not depend on hover.
